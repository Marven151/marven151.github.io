Book scraperPython · pytest
Scraper
python scrape.py --category travel --details

Pulls a book catalogue from a scraping-practice site and saves it as CSV, JSON or Excel. If the site doesn't answer it tries again, and it pauses between requests so it doesn't hammer the server. The parser has tests, so a change to the page layout won't break it silently.

Sheet mergePython · pandas
Script
python sheet_merge.py orders/ --dayfirst shop_b_orders.xlsx

Three shops, three different exports: other column names, stray spaces, duplicates. The script merges them into one sheet and adds a list of what it fixed. Dates written as 05/03 cause the most trouble, since nobody can tell May from March, so you can tell it which file writes the day first.

How we work together

Sample first
For a data job I first send a free sample of the first rows from your source, so you can check the columns. For a website, a sketch of the home page. You pay only once the sample or sketch suits you. If it doesn't, we part ways at no cost.
Starting prices
A custom scraper with a clean CSV, Excel or JSON file from $99. A business lead list from public sources, $79 per 500 records. Monitoring from $199 plus $49 a month. A one-page website from $450. The exact price comes by e-mail before I start and doesn't change while the scope stays the same.
Timing
Most data jobs are delivered in 2–5 days. For a website I show the first version within 5 working days of agreeing the scope. The exact date comes with the estimate.
Payment
Jobs under $300: you pay in full on delivery, after you've checked the data against the sample. Larger jobs: 30% deposit, the rest on approval, before I hand over the files. Card, PayPal or bank transfer; you get an invoice for every job.
Backing out
If the full dataset doesn't match the agreed sample, you don't pay for it. For a website, if you don't want to continue after the first version, I refund the deposit in full within 7 days.
Approval
You have 7 days to comment on a delivery. If you don't get back to me, I pause the work until you do.
Ownership
Once you've paid in full, the script, the data and the website are yours.
Changes
Two rounds of changes are included. Further ones I quote separately, up front.
Files and domain
You get every file. For a website you register the domain in your own name, and connecting it is a $25 extra. You provide the texts and photos unless we agree otherwise.
Bigger projects
Tools, monitoring and apps need looking after once delivered. I agree that separately and up front: a monthly fee or changes at a set rate.
What I don't scrape
Nothing behind a login, nothing that needs a CAPTCHA or a block worked around, and no personal data of EU residents. Public business data, prices, listings and catalogues only, at a polite request rate.

The figures in the estimate are starting prices for small projects. I give the exact price by e-mail before I start, and it doesn't change while the scope stays the same.

What you can check yourself: a site that works on a phone and has https, a Lighthouse score you can measure without me, and a price that doesn't grow while the scope stays the same. I don't promise first place on Google or a set number of customers, because nobody can honestly guarantee that.

Whose is the code, and what if I disappear?

Yours. You get every file, and I can put the site on your own account, on a domain registered to you. Nothing depends on me, so any developer can make the next changes without my help.

Where will the site live?

On free hosting that allows business sites by default, e.g. Cloudflare Pages or Netlify, set up in your name. If you already have hosting or a domain, I'll connect it for $25.

And if something needs changing after delivery?

Small fixes are included. Bigger changes I quote separately, also up front.

Is scraping legal?

I only take what anyone can see on a public page without logging in, I don't work around CAPTCHAs or blocks, and I don't collect personal data: a lead list holds company details, not people's. I keep request rates low so I don't load anyone's server. If something looks doubtful I'll say so straight away.

Poland, Central European Time. I answer every e-mail within one working day, so a message from the US gets a reply by your next morning.

By card through a payment link, by PayPal, or by bank transfer, in US dollars or euros. You get an invoice for every job.
